Sweep StuffFiles

Upload files from your local StuffFiles folder; the server matches each one to its record and slot. Files already on the matched record aren't overwritten — they're listed as skipped.

  • Desktop: click Choose folder and pick your StuffFiles folder. The browser uploads everything inside, keeping the path structure for matching.
  • iPhone / iPad: the iOS Files app doesn't expose folder upload. Pick a category below, then tap Choose files and select files from one folder at a time. Group is read from the filename when present, otherwise the optional Group hint below.

Delete-sync requires uploading the affected record's whole folder (with its _data.json) so the server has a baseline. Records without a snapshot in the upload are never auto-deleted. Identity slots (cover image, license front/back, etc.) are also protected. Capped at 50 deletions per batch.
