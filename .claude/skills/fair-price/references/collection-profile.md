# Collection profile — gap matching for the offer scanner

Maintained by hand (Mark or Claude). The offer scanner treats **Collecting
focus** areas as standing interests and **Wanted / gaps** entries as holes to
fill: a fair-or-better price on a listed gap is a strong pursue; a bargain on
a duplicate is only worth a mention. When this file is stale or silent about
a category, judge offers on value alone and say gap-matching was skipped.

## Standing rules

- Grade floor: **55 (About Uncirculated) is as low as Mark goes** — prefer
  UNC / Choice UNC and better for type notes. The ONLY exception: genuine
  rarity, where the census shows essentially nothing available at higher
  grades; then a lower-grade example may be surfaced, with the population
  evidence stated.
- No price ceiling. Mark decides on price himself — the scanner's job is
  the fair-value evidence and the verdict, never a budget gate. "Worth a
  look" means the evidence is thin or the call is genuinely his, not that
  a number crossed some line.
- Never a duplicate unless it is a grade upgrade at a fair price.
